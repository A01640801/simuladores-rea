02 / Build your model

ARIMA uses recent history. SARIMA adds a repeating seasonal rhythm.

Monthly + annual rhythm → 12. Daily + weekly rhythm → 7. Frequency alone does not guarantee a seasonal pattern.

Change one setting at a time, then fit. Orders specify how many lags to use; the model estimates their weights.
